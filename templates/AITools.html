🔐 Use AI Responsibly

  • Do not blindly trust every AI answer.
  • Verify important information from reliable sources.
  • Do not share passwords or sensitive personal information.
  • Use AI as a helper, not as a replacement for your own thinking.
  • Follow copyright and academic rules when using AI-generated content.
