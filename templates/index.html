Awareness and Use of AI Tools Among Students

Understanding how Artificial Intelligence is changing the way we learn, work, create and solve problems.

INTRODUCTION

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks can include understanding language, recognizing images, finding patterns, making predictions, generating content and solving problems

AI Around Us

📱Smartphones:-Face recognition, voice assistants, camera enhancement and personalized suggestions

🎬 Entertainment:- Netflix, YouTube and music platforms use recommendation systems to suggest content based on user activity

🛒 Online Shopping:- AI can analyze browsing and purchasing patterns to provide personalized recommendations

🚗 Transportation:-AI is used in navigation, traffic prediction, driver-assistance systems and route optimization

🏥 Healthcare:- AI can assist with medical-image analysis, research and identifying patterns in health data

💻 Software Development:-AI tools can help developers generate code, explain errors, write tests and document software

✨ Why AI Awareness Matters

As AI becomes part of everyday life, understanding how to use it responsibly is becoming increasingly important. AI can improve productivity, support learning, assist decision-making and help create new possibilities. At the same time, users need to understand its limitations, verify important information and protect their privacy

🎯 About THIS Project

This project explores awareness and use of AI tools, with a focus on how people interact with AI, what they use it for, its benefits, challenges and the importance of responsible AI usage
